# Editor's Proof Sheet
**Idea:** The page is a printer's proof being marked up by an editor: warm paper, a slanted route line in red pencil that zigzags down the sheet, stopping at three circled decisions. The reader watches taste being exercised, not described.

**From the string:**
- 64 characters → an 8-column grid with 8px base unit; content sits on columns 2 to 7, marginalia in the outer ones.
- Recurring Z/z (zez, ZSs, Zd, Rz) → the route line moves in Z-shaped diagonals; sections alternate left and right along it, asymmetrically.
- Bookend capitals I...I and the W-A-W-A run → a sharp, pointed accent: tick marks and caret symbols, sawtooth rules between sections.
- Sparse digits (8, 3, 1, 5, 3, 2, 1, 1, 7) among letters → numbers are rare and precious: only three numbered gates, plus the one big figure, 162.
- Confusable l/I/1 glyphs throughout → a monospace with clearly distinguished glyphs for labels and the command.

**Look:** Bone paper (#EFE8DA), ink of deep bottle green (#16322A), one red-pencil vermilion (#D8432B) used only for the route and marks. No indigo, no grey. Headlines in a high-contrast serif (Fraunces-like, large, tightly set, left-aligned); body in a humanist sans; `/friday` and labels in a monospace. Texture: faint paper grain, ruled proof margins with crop marks. One hand-drawn SVG circle style for emphasis. No cards, no logos.

**Signature moment:** While scrolling, the vermilion line draws itself in diagonal strokes, pauses to circle each gate, and a tiny "kept" tick lands. At the Variant gate four unlabelled thumbnails sit side by side; hover reveals only the circled pick, never the model. Reduced motion: the line is simply drawn.

**How it says the three messages:**
- Taste: the page is an edited artefact; restraint, one accent, and every mark placed on purpose.
- Builds own tools: a margin note shows the 162-piece ruling list collapsing to what survived (keep, cut, merge, replace), then the built-in-the-gaps parts in red.
- Helps teams: the same route line is redrawn over a blank "your team's workflow" sheet, with the gates marked as questions.

**Ending:** The route line ends at a signature line on the sheet: "Show me your team's workflow." Email and LinkedIn appear as two handwritten-style links on the line, with no button and no form.
