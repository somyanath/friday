# Proof Sheet
**Idea:** The homepage is an editor's marked-up proof: warm paper, a narrow left text column, and a wide right margin full of pencil annotations, circled choices and proofreader's marks. Friday is shown as the process that decides, and the page itself is visibly decided.

**From the string:**
- Two lone 1s bracketing a short run (1…1) → a bracket motif; each of the three gates is framed in square brackets, and the text column is bracketed by a thin rule on the left only.
- A long capital run at the start, then a mostly lowercase tail → the page opens dense and loud (big display type), then relaxes into open, quiet running text.
- Digits appear only six times, in the order 1,1,9,5,7,3 → hue 57 for the paper (warm ochre-cream) and 195 for the ink (deep teal-black); the complement of 195 gives one vermilion mark colour.
- Repeated "i" and "j" with dots → dots are the only ornament: three dots stand for the three gates and recur as bullets and the progress mark.
- Uneven, ragged rhythm with no mirrored halves → deliberately asymmetric layout, nothing centred.

**Look:** Paper oklch(96% .02 90), ink oklch(24% .04 195), one vermilion (hue 15) used only for marks, never for fills. Display in a high-contrast serif with italics (Newsreader or Fraunces), commands and labels in a quiet mono. Text column about 34rem, flush left; margin notes in italic at 0.85 size sit right on desktop and fold beneath their paragraph on phone. Imagery is hand-drawn SVG marks (circles, strike-throughs, carets, a stet), plus the four variants shown as simple paper thumbnails, not screenshots of source. No gradients, no shadows, a faint paper grain at 3 percent.

**Signature moment:** At the Variant gate, four thumbnails sit side by side. As they scroll into view, a vermilion pencil circle draws itself around one, the other three dim, and a margin note writes in: "picked blind." Reduced motion shows the circle already drawn.

**How it says the three messages:**
- Taste: restraint, one accent colour, and a layout that makes decisions the way the page says Somya does.
- Builds own tools: a margin strike-through list shows 162 pieces from three collections, each marked keep, cut, merge or replace, with the survivors forming Friday.
- Helps teams: the cockpit-versus-process contrast is annotated as "what your agents run" against "what decides it", then a note reads "same skill, your team's workflow."

**Ending:** The last margin note is a question in italic: "Where does your team's workflow slow down?" Beneath it, email and LinkedIn are set as underlined serif links, closed by a small vermilion stet mark.
