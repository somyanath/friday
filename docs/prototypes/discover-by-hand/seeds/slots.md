# Slots for the SSoT-style round

Each slot is picked by a script: the 64-character seed is cut into five segments (13, 13, 13, 13, 12 characters), each segment's character codes are summed, and the sum mod the slot's option count (plus one) picks the option. The concept slot indexes the 55-item idea list.

## Palette (8)
1. Dark ground with one saturated signal colour
2. Cyanotype: Prussian blue and white only
3. Primary blocks: red, yellow, blue and black
4. Earth: olive, rust, clay and bone
5. High-key pastels on white
6. Black and white only, no colour at all
7. Phosphor: neon on near-black, like a CRT
8. Two-colour risograph overprint (fluorescent pink and teal)

## Type (8)
1. Huge grotesk sans, Swiss
2. Monospace throughout
3. High-contrast serif display
4. Slab serif or wood type
5. Expressive variable display face
6. Condensed industrial sans, like signage
7. Hand lettering as accent over a neutral sans
8. Pixel or bitmap face

## Layout (8)
1. Single horizontal scroll
2. Strict asymmetric Swiss grid
3. One pinned full-screen scene that changes on scroll
4. Long single text-led column
5. Broken grid, collage
6. Split screen, two halves
7. Dense panels, many small modules
8. Full-bleed bands in sequence

## Motion (8)
1. Scroll-scrubbed sequence
2. Typing or terminal replay
3. Physics: things fall, stack, collide
4. Hard cuts, instant swaps, no tweening
5. Lines drawing themselves
6. A live comparison wipe
7. Slow ambient drift
8. Almost none: one moment only
