# Pause Light

**Idea:** A calm mission-control wall rendered as one long column of condensed signage-type statements, each followed by a slim telemetry strip (route line, gate status, two-family check). A single red PAUSE lamp sits fixed at the top edge and lights only when the page reaches a place where the human decides.

**Override:** none

**Look:** Off-white paper stock is not used; the ground is matte black with a warm bone text colour. Pure signal red is reserved for the PAUSE lamp and the three gate markers; one yellow (hazard-tape yellow) marks "running/agent" states; one blue marks the second model family. Type is a tall condensed grotesque (Barlow Condensed / Oswald class), set huge and uppercase for statements at about 8vw, with a tabular mono-width condensed for readouts. One text column, left-aligned, ragged, with a narrow gutter of status chips pinned to its left edge. Hairline rules, no cards, no shadows, no rounded corners. Texture: faint stencil registration marks, nothing else.

**Signature moment:** In the Variant gate section, a draggable vertical wipe splits the screen: left side the generic page that agents produce alone ("same page four times"), right side a seeded, distinct page. Scrubbing the wipe reveals taste, and auto-sweeps once on entry. The PAUSE lamp flicks red as the wipe is touched, because the human is choosing.

**How it says the three messages:**
- Taste: restraint and signage discipline, with colour used as signal only; the wipe makes the reader pick the better page themselves.
- Builds own tools: the 162 pieces ruled keep/cut/merge/replace appear as a counter ticking down to a few kept, with "curated, not collected" and the cockpit-versus-process contrast stated flatly.
- Helps teams: the three gates and always-pause list are framed as "where your team's humans stop", so the reader substitutes their own workflow.

**Ending:** The PAUSE lamp goes steady red and the last line reads "Your team's workflow: pause here." Two large condensed buttons, Email and LinkedIn, sit under it, framed around speeding up their team.
