# The Proof Sheet
**Idea:** The homepage is an editor's marked-up proof: warm unbleached stock, one ochre highlighter and a green-black pen, with margin notes doing the talking. The page shows taste by editing itself in front of the reader.

**From the string:**
- Digits run 1,5,4,8,9,7,9,4,1, a near-mirror around a 7 → page pacing swells and recedes like a waveform: a tiny opening line, dense middle, tiny closing line. The mirror is deliberately broken (5 vs 8), so nothing is symmetric.
- Digits 1-4-9 as proportions → the column grid is uneven, roughly 1 : 4 : 9 (narrow margin-note rail, mid text, wide specimen area).
- Hex-like fragment "Ec5" → highlighter ochre (about #E8C550), the only accent.
- Long lowercase runs broken by lone capitals → sentence-case copy, with single capitalised words struck out in ochre as editing marks.
- Seven digits fall between the 7th and 64th characters; length 64 = 8x8 → an 8px baseline unit and 8 thumbnails in the narrowing sequence.

**Look:** Paper #F2EBDD, ink #17211C, ochre as highlight only. Newsreader (large italic statements, tight tracking) with a small mono for margin labels. Text sits left and off-centre. A hairline rule runs down the page like a proof margin. Imagery is small greyscale page thumbnails with crop marks, no photos, no cards, no logos. Corners square.

**Signature moment:** In the Direction gate, eight near-identical page thumbnails appear. As the reader scrolls, ochre highlighter strokes strike out five of them, one at a time with a small scrub of clip-path, leaving three. These then slide apart into three different running pages side by side, with the model labels hidden behind a small redaction bar. Under reduced motion, the end state is shown still.

**How it says the three messages:**
- Taste: the restraint and the editing marks are the proof; copy is short and cut hard.
- Builds own tools: a margin note ledger of 162 pieces, each ruled keep, cut, merge or replace, with the missing parts marked "built".
- Helps teams: the final marginal note turns the same red-pen process outward: "your workflow, same pass."

**Ending:** The page narrows to one line of large italic: "Send me your team's slowest handoff." The email link is ochre-underlined and LinkedIn sits beside it in mono. No form, no footer clutter.
