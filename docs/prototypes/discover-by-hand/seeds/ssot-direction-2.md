# Two Staves, One Pencil
**Idea:** The whole page is one long manuscript of a single piece, scrolling sideways. Claude is the upper stave in pink, GPT the lower in teal, and a graphite-grey conductor's pencil (the critic) marks corrections onto both. Friday's process is the music.

**Override:** none

**Look:**
- Warm uncoated paper (#F4EFE4). Only two inks, fluorescent pink (#FF3E8A) and teal (#00A99D), printed with slight misregistration. Where they overlap they multiply into a deep violet-navy. That overlap is the only "third colour", and it appears exactly where the two model families agree or check each other.
- Type is a high-contrast Didone display (Playfair Display or Bodoni Moda, hairlines against heavy stems) in huge italic for section titles, set like movement markings: *Allegro*, *Con gusto*. Body is a plain small serif text face, never a sans.
- Layout is one horizontal scroll: five-line staves run the full width with clefs, bar lines and bar numbers. Each Friday idea is a "movement" (bar group). The three gates are fermatas, held notes with a heavy hairline pause mark. Text sits on, above or under staves, never in cards.
- Texture is halftone grain, riso speckle, and ink-density variation. No drop shadows, no icons. The pencil marks are thin, wobbly, uneven strokes in dark grey.

**Signature moment:** The staves slowly drift, drifting a few pixels against each other so the pink and teal lines creep in and out of registration. As you scroll, a single pencil mark draws itself across a bar: the critic strikes a note on one stave and the other family's note replaces it, in a slow handwritten stroke. At the Variant gate, four notes sit stacked, unlabelled (blind), and a fermata holds over them. Motion is calm and ambient, and it stops under reduced-motion.

**How it says the three messages:**
- Taste: the craft of the score itself (Didone scale, true overprint, restraint to two inks) is the proof, and the page is not symmetrical, since the bars breathe unevenly.
- Builds own tools: the opening is a "key signature" of three respected collections, with the 162 pieces shown as notes, each marked kept, cut, merged or replaced, and the missing parts written in by hand.
- Helps teams: a late "tutti" bar where both staves play together and the pencil turns outward. The line reads: the same score, a bigger orchestra. It shows the reader how the same process fits their team, which is where the jump to the reader's team is made.

**Ending:** The final bar is a double barline and a blank stave. Email and LinkedIn are written as two handwritten notes in pink and teal, headed "Your team's workflow?", and they overprint into violet when hovered or tapped.
