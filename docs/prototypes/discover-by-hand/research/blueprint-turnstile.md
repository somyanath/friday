# Blueprint Turnstile: research

**Process behind the look:** Herschel's cyanotype (1842) was sold for drawing reprography from 1872 as "ferro-prussiate" paper and dominated until the 1940s. Paper is coated with ferric ammonium citrate and potassium ferricyanide, a translucent ink-on-vellum original is contact-printed under UV, and it is developed in plain water. Light turns exposed paper into insoluble Prussian blue; unexposed salts wash out. So the drawing is a negative: white lines on blue, because the pen lines blocked the light. Quirks that follow: no greys, only blue or paper-white; no colour at all; the blue darkens as it dries; the print is the same size as the original.

**Colour, texture, composition:** Colour is one blue plus paper. Texture is uneven coating and brush-edge margins. Composition is a ruled border, a zone grid in the margin (letters up the side, numbers along the edge), a title block in the bottom right corner, and drawn views set in the field with generous empty space.

**Rules:**
1. Exactly two inks: Prussian blue and white. Hairlines are thin white, never grey.
2. All marks are line, not fill. No shadows, gradients or images.
3. Line weight has about three steps: object line, hairline, border.
4. Every dimension line sits outside the object, with extension lines and ticks; shorter dimensions nearest the object; dimension lines never cross.
5. Border, zone references and a title block anchor the sheet. The title block is bottom right.
6. Headlines flush-left, ragged-right, on a visible grid; text never centred.
7. Scale contrast does the hierarchy: one huge size, one tiny size, little between.
8. Asymmetry: large empty blue is deliberate; no mirrored layout.
9. Annotations are capped uppercase, tabular figures, one family.
10. Mark a change with one revision cloud, once.

**Named patterns:** Title block (drawing no., scale, drawn/checked/approved, date). Zone grid or zoning (margin letters and numbers). Border. Dimension line with extension lines and tick or arrow terminators. Leader line with shoulder (arrowhead, dot or loop end). Revision cloud with a revision symbol triangle. Revision block (SYM, ZONE, DESCRIPTION, DATE, APPROVAL). Swiss: modular grid, flush-left ragged-right, asymmetric layout, Akzidenz-Grotesk, Univers, Helvetica.

**Where this Direction risks a cliché:** "Blueprint" is the default tech-startup metaphor, so it reads as a template unless the sheet carries real title-block data and honest dimension logic. huge grotesk on dark is also a current AI-landing default.
