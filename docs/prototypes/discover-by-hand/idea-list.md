# Idea list

1. Gate Boss Doors: a scrolling map where each gate is a heavy door the reader walks through.
2. Railway Signal Box: three gates as trackside signals; Variants run as parallel trains, a human throws the switch.
3. Newspaper Front Page: broadsheet with columns, headlines and a dateline; the gates are editorial desks.
4. Telegraph Wire: the page arrives as terse telegram strips, STOP between sentences, contact is the reply slip.
5. Letterpress Broadside: heavy wood-type posters, ink-bleed texture, one bold sheet per message.
6. Card Catalogue Drawer: pull out index cards for each of the 162 rulings (keep, cut, merge, replace).
7. Courtroom Transcript: critic and builder models cross-examine each other; Somya is the judge at three gates.
8. Airport Departures Board: split-flap board listing tasks, routes, and gate status; the gates are literal gates.
9. Darkroom Photo Print: the page develops from blank, images emerge in a chemical tray as you scroll.
10. Four Lanes Race Track: four Variants run side by side as lanes, scrolling reveals the winner picked blind.
11. Blind Taste Test: the reader picks between unlabeled page fragments, then learns which model built each.
12. Radical Asymmetry Collage: torn-edge cut-paper layout, text at angles, deliberately off-grid but balanced.
13. Giant Type Only: the page is nothing but enormous typography in one expressive typeface, with minimal imagery.
14. Terminal That Talks: the whole page is a live `/friday` session replaying a task start to merge.
15. Command Palette Homepage: press cmd-K to navigate; the page is a search box and results.
16. Choose Your Route: reader types a task, the page shows the one-line route Friday would show.
17. Cockpit Versus Autopilot: split page, left raw cockpit of windows, right the process sitting on top of it.
18. Orchestral Score: sheet music where Claude and GPT are two staves, the critic is the conductor's pencil.
19. Chess Match Notation: two model families as players, moves annotated, human as arbiter at three gates.
20. Medieval Illuminated Manuscript: gilded initials, marginalia doodles, gates as illustrated archways.
21. Blueprint Drawing: cyanotype technical drawing with dimensions and callouts for the workflow.
22. Museum Exhibition Wall: placards, plinths and wall labels; each Variant hangs as a framed piece.
23. Isometric Workshop Floor: a quiet isometric room with benches, one per agent; click a bench to zoom.
24. Seed Packet Garden: seed strings literally planted; four different plants grow from four seeds.
25. Slot Machine Of Directions: pull the lever, a fresh wildly different design direction lands each time.
26. Page That Redesigns Itself: reload gives a different Variant of the homepage, with a swap-direction control.
27. Four Pages Stacked: the homepage is four real different homepages overlaid, peel layers to compare.
28. Slider Between Variants: a draggable divider wipes between two complete designs live.
29. Vinyl Record Sleeve: sleeve front, liner notes, tracklist of the gates; spinning disc as scroll indicator.
30. Film Storyboard: comic-like panels with camera directions; generated video as the motion beat.
31. Silent Film Intertitles: black screen, white title cards, piano-roll pacing, contact as the final card.
32. Receipt Tape: a long thermal-paper receipt scrolling out of a printer, itemising each gate (no prices).
33. Instruction Manual Flatpack: IKEA-style wordless line diagrams for assembling the workflow, step by step.
34. Subway Map: stations are gates, lines are model families, interchange stations where humans stop.
35. Stage Play Script: acts and scenes with stage directions; agents are cast, Somya is the audience of one.
36. Lab Notebook: handwritten marginalia, taped-in screenshots, coffee ring, hypotheses and results.
37. Tarot Spread: reader draws cards for each gate, each card an illustrated archetype.
38. Mission Control Wall: dense dashboard of telemetry, but calm, with one red PAUSE light for the human.
39. Gates As Physical Turnstiles: scroll physically blocked at each gate until the reader clicks to approve.
40. Mirror Page: top half and bottom half are two model families, each critiquing the other's text.
41. Redline Document: the page is a contract-style draft with strikethroughs showing cut and merge rulings.
42. Kintsugi Cracks: cracks of gold run through the layout where work was sent back and repaired.
43. Heavy Brutalist Raw HTML: system fonts, hard borders, default blue links, deliberately anti-design confidence.
44. Pure Emoji Page: every concept is one emoji and nothing else.
45. Everything Centred Gradient Hero: the banned AI-tell page, played straight as a joke that turns into the real page.
46. Single Endless Horizontal Scroll: the page runs sideways like a scroll painting.
47. Zoom Into One Pixel: start on a single screen pixel and zoom out through layers to the workflow.
48. Sound-Led Page: each section has an ambient audio tone, muted by default, with visual waveform.
49. Weather Report: forecast metaphor, fronts of ideas colliding, pressure rising before each gate.
50. Fax Machine Contact: contact section arrives as a fax print with a stamp and curling paper.
51. Bookbinder's Signature: pages that fold and turn like a printed pamphlet with visible spine.
52. Variable Font Living Type: letterforms morph weight and width as the reader scrolls through the process.
53. Cursor-Lit Dark Room: page is dark, only a flashlight cursor reveals content near it.
54. Trading Cards: each of the three skill collections as a collectible card with stats and rulings.
55. Field Guide Naturalist Plate: species-style plates of the four Variants, with Latin names and annotations.
